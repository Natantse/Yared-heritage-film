# Design and Audience Strategy

## 1. Project Purpose

The purpose of this project is to introduce international audiences to Saint Yared and the heritage of Ethiopian sacred music through a short cinematic documentary.

The film explores how sacred music connects faith, history, cultural identity, and generations.

## 2. Intended Audience

The primary audience is international viewers who may have little prior knowledge of Ethiopian musical and religious traditions.

Secondary audiences include:
- People interested in Ethiopian history and culture.
- Documentary and world-music enthusiasts.
- Students interested in cultural heritage.
- Viewers interested in AI-assisted filmmaking.

## 3. Audience Learning Goals

After watching the film, viewers should:

1. Recognize Saint Yared as an important figure in Ethiopian sacred musical tradition.
2. Understand that Ethiopian sacred music is connected to faith and cultural identity.
3. Appreciate how musical traditions are preserved and passed between generations.
4. Become interested in learning more about Ethiopian heritage.

## 4. Creative Direction

The film uses a cinematic historical atmosphere.

Visual elements include:
- Ethiopian highlands and landscapes.
- Ancient stone churches.
- Sacred manuscripts.
- Ethiopian Orthodox worship.
- An artistic interpretation of Saint Yared.

The narration is in Amharic, with English subtitles for international accessibility.

The soundtrack is an AI-generated instrumental inspired by the Ethiopian begena.

## 5. Design Decisions

| Design decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| Amharic narration | Preserves the cultural and linguistic character of the story |
| English subtitles | Makes the film accessible to international audiences |
| Historical visual style | Supports the subject and atmosphere |
| Slow cinematic pacing | Gives viewers time to absorb the imagery |
| Begenа-inspired music | Connects the soundtrack to Ethiopian musical heritage |
| Vertical and horizontal formats | Supports different viewing platforms |

## 6. Audience Testing Plan

The assignment calls for testing with at least three people.

The planned tests are:

**Test 1 — Radio cut**
- Play the narration and subtitle version.
- Ask viewers whether the narration is clear.
- Check whether the English subtitles are readable and understandable.

**Test 2 — Full film**
- Show the completed film.
- Ask viewers what they learned about Saint Yared.
- Ask whether the visuals and music support the story.

**Test status:** Viewer feedback has not yet been documented. Results and changes will be added after the tests are completed. No audience feedback is claimed without conducting the tests.

## 7. Evaluation Criteria

The film will be evaluated based on:

- Clarity of the story.
- Accuracy and respect in representing cultural heritage.
- Readability and timing of English subtitles.
- Audio quality and narration clarity.
- Visual consistency.
- Accessibility for international audiences.

## 8. Future Improvements

Possible improvements include:
- Testing with a wider international audience.
- Improving subtitle synchronization based on viewer feedback.
- Adding more historical references and context.
- Using authentic recorded Ethiopian instruments in a future version.
