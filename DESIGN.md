# Design Document: Yared – The Voice of Ethiopian Heritage

## 1. Film Concept
This 60–90 second heritage film introduces Saint Yared and his importance in Ethiopian sacred musical tradition.

## 2. Target Audience
The target audience is international audiences, especially people interested in world history, music, religion, and cultural heritage.

## 3. Purpose
The purpose is to introduce viewers to Saint Yared and show how Ethiopian sacred music helps preserve cultural identity and tradition.

## 4. Central Message
Music is more than sound. It carries faith, history, identity, and cultural memory from one generation to another.

## 5. Visual Direction
The film uses traditional Ethiopian painting and illuminated manuscript inspiration. The visual palette includes gold, deep blue, red, and earth tones.

The images are artistic interpretations, not verified historical portraits.

## 6. Sound and Language
- Narration: Amharic
- Subtitles: English
- Music: Original score created for this film
- Sound design: Music will remain softer than narration so the words are clear.

## 7. Story Structure
1. Introduce the Ethiopian highlands and the importance of music.
2. Present Saint Yared and his traditional historical significance.
3. Show Ethiopian church music and its transmission.
4. Connect the heritage to present generations.
5. End with the message that Yared remains a voice of Ethiopian heritage.

## 8. Intended Audience Response
The film aims to create curiosity, respect, and appreciation for Ethiopian musical heritage.
