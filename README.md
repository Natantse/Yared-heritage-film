# Saint Yared: Ethiopia's Sacred Musical Heritage

## Project Overview

**Saint Yared: The Heritage Short** is an AI-assisted short documentary celebrating the musical and spiritual heritage of Saint Yared and Ethiopian Orthodox sacred music.

The film is designed for international audiences, introducing them to the history, atmosphere, and cultural significance of Ethiopian sacred musical traditions.

## Project Goals

- Introduce international audiences to Saint Yared and his musical legacy.
- Explore the connection between music, faith, history, and cultural identity.
- Present Ethiopian heritage through cinematic storytelling.
- Combine AI-generated visuals, narration, subtitles, and music in a short film.

## Deliverables

- **Heritage film:** A cinematic short film with Amharic narration, English subtitles, and an AI-generated instrumental score.
- **Radio cut:** An audio-focused version of the narration presented with English subtitles on screen.

## Production Process

The project used AI-assisted tools and a combination of editing and media-production software.

The workflow included:

1. Research and script development.
2. Storyboarding and scene planning.
3. AI-assisted image and video generation.
4. Amharic narration generation.
5. AI-assisted instrumental music generation.
6. English subtitle preparation.
7. Video editing, audio mixing, and final export.

## Creative and Technical Approach

The film uses historical imagery, Ethiopian landscapes, church interiors, sacred manuscripts, and an artistic interpretation of Saint Yared.

The narration is in Amharic, with English subtitles to make the story accessible to international viewers.

The instrumental soundtrack is AI-generated and inspired by the Ethiopian begena. It should not be understood as an authentic recording of a traditional begena performance.

## AI Transparency

Some visuals, narration, and music were created with AI tools. Saint Yared's visual representation is an artistic interpretation, not a verified historical portrait.

AI-generated content was reviewed and edited as part of the production process.

## Intended Audience

The primary audience is international viewers interested in Ethiopian culture, history, music, and religious heritage.
