# Final Project Report: Saint Yared – The Heritage of Ethiopian Sacred Music

## 1. Project Overview

This project is an AI-assisted short documentary introducing Saint Yared and the heritage of Ethiopian sacred music to international audiences.

The goal was to communicate the historical, cultural, and spiritual significance of this musical heritage through cinematic storytelling.

## 2. Target Audience

The primary audience is international viewers interested in:
- Ethiopian history and culture
- Religious and sacred music
- African cultural heritage
- Documentary filmmaking

## 3. Production Workflow and AI Tools

The project used several AI-assisted tools:

- **Gemini 3.6 Flash:** Text generation and storytelling support.
- **FLUX.1-schnell:** Visual keyframe generation.
- **Wan 2.1:** AI video generation.
- **Microsoft Edge TTS:** Amharic narration using the Ameha voice.
- **MusicGen:** Begenna-inspired instrumental music.
- **FFmpeg:** Video editing, assembly, and subtitles.
- **Python and JSON Schema:** Storyboard validation.

The documentary was assembled from eight planned shots, with a target duration of approximately 61 seconds.

## 4. Challenges and Solutions

One of the biggest challenges was assembling generated video scenes into a consistent documentary.

Other challenges included:
- Resolving storyboard JSON formatting errors.
- Combining video and audio.
- Creating subtitles.
- Producing different video formats.
- Managing generated files and exports.

Python validation and FFmpeg helped address several of these challenges.

## 5. Ethical Considerations

The project recognizes the importance of representing Ethiopian cultural and religious heritage responsibly.

The generated visual representation of Saint Yared is an artistic interpretation, not an authenticated historical portrait. The instrumental music is begenna-inspired and should not be presented as an authentic historical recording.

AI-generated content should support cultural education without replacing historical research or authentic traditions.

## 6. Learning Outcomes

Through this project, I learned how to:
- Develop an AI-assisted storytelling workflow.
- Generate images and video scenes.
- Create Amharic narration.
- Assemble video and audio using FFmpeg.
- Validate structured storyboard data.
- Document technical decisions and limitations.
- Consider cultural authenticity and responsible AI use.

## 7. Limitations and Future Improvements

Future improvements include:
- Completing and verifying the horizontal documentary export.
- Conducting audience testing with at least three viewers and documenting their feedback.
- Improving visual consistency and historical accuracy.
- Adding further verified historical sources.
- Improving accessibility through subtitles and narration.

## 8. Project Deliverables

- Vertical documentary MP4.
- Radio cut with English subtitles.
- GitHub source repository and project documentation.
- Walkthrough recording.
- GitHub repository screenshot.

The horizontal documentary export remains pending verification.

## 9. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how AI-assisted creative tools can support storytelling about Ethiopian cultural heritage. It also highlights the importance of human judgment, cultural sensitivity, transparency, and technical validation when creating AI-assisted documentaries.
